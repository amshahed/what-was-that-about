## Chapter by chapter

### Ch. 1 — [Ubik used car]
- June 1992, New York, 3:30 a.m. Hollis' top telepath, S. Dole Melipone, drops off the tracking map
  at Runciter Associates. Many Hollis psis have been missing for two months.
- Runciter flies to the Beloved Brethren Moratorium in Zurich to ask his dead wife Ella what to do.
  Owner Herbert Schoenheit von Vogelsang explains the half-life basics: cold-pac caskets, a limited
  number of revivals, a "lading" sheet that shows days left.

### Ch. 2 — [Ubik beer]
- Ella (dead at 20) talks to Runciter from her casket. She dreams of a smoky red light.
- Mid-talk, a boy's voice takes over the line: **Jory**, a 15-year-old half-lifer in the next casket.
  Half-lifers who are stored close together leak into each other.
- Runciter orders Ella isolated. Von Vogelsang thinks it may be too late. Runciter never gets his
  advice.

### Ch. 3 — [Ubik instant coffee]
- **Joe Chip** at home: broke, hungover, credit rating "quadruple G". His **front door asks for five
  cents** and threatens to sue when he tries to unscrew it.
- Scout G. G. Ashwood brings a new find: **Pat Conley**, 19, from a Topeka kibbutz. Her talent: she
  changes the past by thinking about it.
- Joe's test gives a strange result. Pat shows him a report he wrote in a version of the morning that
  no longer exists. Joe writes a glowing report, but he marks it with the secret symbol that means
  "dangerous". Pat moves into his apartment.

### Ch. 4 — [Ubik salad dressing]
- Runciter is back in New York. Zoe Wirt (head "like a basketball") offers a big contract from the
  tycoon **Stanton Mick**: Hollis psis have got into his secret project on Luna.
- Runciter hires Pat even though he sees Joe's danger symbol.

### Ch. 5 — [Ubik stomach remedy]
- Runciter picks his best eleven inertials (the comic fashion parade: Edie, Francesca, Tippy, Jon,
  Al, Tito, Don, Sammy, Wendy, Fred, Pat).
- Pat shows her power: the morning rewrites itself. Runciter is suddenly retired, then Pat is
  suddenly Joe's wife. She puts it all back, but she keeps the wedding ring.
- Several inertials say they dreamed of two Hollis men, "Matt and Bill".
- Fourteen fly to Luna: the eleven, Joe, Runciter and Zoe Wirt.

### Ch. 6 — [Ubik razor blade]
- In the underground hotel on Luna, Joe measures the field: there is **no psi field at all**. It is
  a trap.
- "Stanton Mick" walks in, floats to the ceiling and explodes. He was a humanoid bomb.
- Runciter is mortally hurt. The others have cuts and burns. Joe takes command. They put Runciter in
  the ship's cold-pac and fly to Earth.
- First signs of decay: Joe's cigarette crumbles to dust. Wendy says, "I feel old."

### Ch. 7 — [Ubik non-stick coating]
- **The odd paragraph:** for a few lines, the text shows Runciter standing in front of Joe's frozen
  body in a casket. Then it snaps back to Joe. (A planted clue.)
- More decay at Zurich: sour cream, stale cigarettes, money that turns into old, withdrawn coins.
- Joe calls Hollis, who seems to expect the call.
- The moratorium can't reach Runciter; there is only a hum. Joe becomes acting head of the firm.

### Ch. 8 — [Ubik savings & loan]
- In his hotel room, Joe hears Runciter's voice on the phone, talking about firm business.
- **Wendy is found in the closet:** a tiny, dried-out husk.
- Back in New York, Al lists the strange things. Runciter's face is now on the coins and bills. A
  matchbook ad says Runciter is "frozen in cold-pac".

### Ch. 9 — [Ubik hair conditioner]
- In Baltimore, Joe finds a note in Runciter's handwriting in a cigarette carton.
- A new tape recorder is suddenly a 40-year-old model, "Made by Runciter of Zurich", service in Des
  Moines (Runciter's home town).
- Al sees a 1910 open-cage elevator. In a men's room he finds graffiti in Runciter's hand: **"I'm the
  one that's alive. You're all dead."** Al works it out: the bomb killed them, not Runciter.
- Al gets weak and cold and dies alone in the dark men's room.

### Ch. 10 — [Ubik deodorant]
- TV news: Runciter's body has been moved to a mortuary in Des Moines.
- Runciter appears in a **Ubik TV commercial** that seems to talk to Joe. It says "I'm dead" and
  tells him to buy Ubik.
- Joe's apartment has regressed to **1939** (Atwater-Kent radio, Victrola, a gas stove, a 1939
  paper). Only his coin door is still there.
- The free Ubik sample has turned into "liver and kidney balm", then an old "Elixir of Ubique". A
  hidden note tells Joe not to give up.
- Joe hires biplane pilot Sandy Jespersen to fly him to Des Moines.

### Ch. 11 — [Ubik sleeping pill]
- Des Moines, 1939. Mortician Mr. Bliss is a bigot and a 1939 isolationist. Joe "predicts" WWII.
- Joe meets the rest of the group. Francesca tells her dream: the hand of God opens to show a spray
  can marked UBIK.
- Joe opens Runciter's casket: the body has been rotting for a long time.
- Edie is missing. Joe says it out loud: "We were all killed in the blast."

### Ch. 12 — [Ubik toaster pastry]
- A motorcycle cop gives Joe a ticket. Runciter's message is on it: "you are in greater danger…
  what Pat Conley said is…" It is cut off. It sends him to Archer's Drugstore.
- The drugstore flickers between a 1992 shop and an old pharmacy. Joe gets in, but Ubik is $40 and
  he can't pay. The label says Pat is lying.
- Skywriting: "KEEP THE OLD SWIZER UP, JOE!"
- Edie is dead. Joe and Don decide Pat is behind everything. Joe accuses her.

### Ch. 13 — [Ubik bra]
- Joe starts to die: cold, weak, in a red-tinted dim world.
- He climbs the hotel stairs for his life. Pat climbs beside him, light and mocking. She "confesses":
  she worked with Ashwood and Hollis, and she killed Wendy.
- Joe crawls into Room 203. **Runciter is there.** He sprays Joe with Ubik and the cold goes away.

### Ch. 14 — [Ubik plastic wrap]
- Runciter tells the truth: **he is alive. They are in cold-pac**, wired together as a group at the
  moratorium. He has tried to reach them for a week.
- Pat is in cold-pac too. Sammy Mundo is in a coma in a Zurich hospital.
- Runciter blames Pat's talent, then admits he doesn't know what Ubik is. Something on "their side"
  gave it to him.
- Short shift to Runciter in the real world: he worries that Jory is feeding on Ella.

### Ch. 15 — [Ubik mouthwash]
- "Don Denny" returns with a doctor, sprays the last of the Ubik on himself and reveals himself as
  **Jory**.
- Jory explains: he eats what is left of half-lifers. He ate Wendy, the real Don, Pat, the others.
  He built this world for them. He can't stop it regressing. He let them blame Pat for fun.
- Jory bites Joe's hand. "You can't kill me."

### Ch. 16 — [Ubik cereal]
- On the street Joe meets **Ella** (blond pigtails, red skirt). She will soon be reborn.
- She gives him a certificate for a lifetime supply of Ubik. She wants him to take her place as
  Runciter's adviser. She says there are Jorys in every moratorium.
- The drugstore man is Jory again; the Ubik there has regressed. Joe collapses.
- A woman "from the Ubik factory" sprays him and gives a fake-science explanation (a negative
  ionizer). Ubik was made by half-lifers, mostly Ella.

### Ch. 17 — [no ad: Ubik speaks as God]
- Runciter, outside at the moratorium, tips an attendant with coins. The attendant asks what kind of
  money it is. The coins show **Joe Chip's face**. "This was just the beginning."

## Short summary for the video

1992. Psychics spy for business. "Anti-psychics" block them for a fee. The dead are kept "half-alive"
in cold storage so their families can call them.

Joe Chip is a broke technician at Glen Runciter's anti-psi firm. A tycoon hires the firm's best eleven
for a job on the Moon. It is a trap: a bomb goes off. Runciter seems to die. Joe and the others rush
him home.

Then the world starts to rot. Cigarettes crumble. Money goes old. Runciter's face shows up on the
coins. Team members dry up and die one by one. Everything slides back to 1939. And messages from
Runciter appear everywhere: graffiti, TV ads, traffic tickets. They tell Joe that he is the dead one.

The twist: the team died in the blast. Runciter survived. They are in half-life, and a dead teenager
called Jory is eating them and building the fake world around them. Runciter's dead wife Ella, and a
spray can called Ubik, help Joe stay alive.

Then the last page: in the "real" world, Runciter finds Joe Chip's face on his coins. So who is
alive?
